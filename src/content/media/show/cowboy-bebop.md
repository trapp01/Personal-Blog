Overflowing with creativity. No two episodes felt the same in terms of vibe and genre. I love how the show being mostly procedural fed into the overall message and character arcs. Just a couple of lonely cowboys going on random side quests to fill the void of family and a place where they belong. The mood of the show is what's stuck with me the most. Super fun watch with an undertone of sadness that gets balanced perfectly.
